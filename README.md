# SPACE RIDERS: Skippy's Quest

A top-down 16-bit action-adventure starring Skippy. Free, in the browser: https://sanyziahj.github.io/skippys-quest/

- Chapter 1: The Desert Star Shard (Santt, ROS, the Sand Dungeon, the Void-Eye Centipede)
- Chapter 2: The Seaport (Cash's Portal Nexus, the Pirate King, Ramshackle the Viking, the Sea Cave, the Void Kraken)
- Chapter 3: Sakura Village → Neon Edge → SuperNova Club (Ronin, the bamboo trial, DeeJay, Draco & Spark)
- Chapter 4: The Andromeda Casino (Silas, blackjack, AL on piano, Silas's Vault, the Void Security Core, the raygun)
- Chapter 5: The Moon Colony (robot uprising, the Drill Sergeant's light armor + seeking missiles, the Moon Station, the Overlord)
- Chapter 6: The Meteor Shower (Blaze blasts a path, Rusty / Buck / Kal fly-bys, the Comet Star Shard)
- Chapter 7: The Crossroad (Santt's space board, Slide's board shop, board fast travel, the Skyway trial)
- Chapter 8: DreamLand Carnival (Spike's lightning, Cavendish's High Striker, Eyereenia, the mirror Dark World, Furn's stun-circle glasses)
- Chapter 9: Fright's Castle (seven shards open the gate, the Torch Hall, Fright, the Red King returns, credits and a downloadable victory poster). The story is complete.

Dialog boxes show each character's portrait.

Controls: Arrows/WASD move, X attack, Z item, Q/Tab swap item, C roll, E/Space talk, Enter map/pause, M mute. Touch controls on mobile. Progress autosaves.
